  • One-time codes: the codes we email you for verification or sign-in are valid for a few minutes and can be used only once.
  • Message delivery: which notification was sent, when, to which email or mobile number, and whether it was delivered.

2.5 Data we do not ask for

We do not ask for ID numbers, payment details or special categories of data (e.g. health). You do not need to create a password.

Warranty messages (by email and [SMS]). 8. Security

  • connections only over HTTPS,
  • sign-in without a password, with a one-time code sent to your email,
  • files (purchase documents, receipts, attachments) are stored in a protected area that is not publicly accessible,
  • attempt limits and bot protection on the forms,
  • team access with personal accounts and two-step verification,
  • an activity log with a digital signature, so that any alteration can be detected,
  • encrypted backups.

No system is completely secure. If a breach affecting you occurs, we will inform the supervisory authority and, where required, you, as the law provides.

9. Your rights

You have the right to: - access your data and receive a copy, - rectify inaccurate details, - erasure, - restrict processing, - data portability, i.e. receive your data in a machine-readable format, - object to processing based on our legitimate interest, - withdraw your consent to news and offers at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal.

How to exercise them: - Yourself, in «My warranties» → «My data»: a copy of all your details (to view, print or save as PDF), a copy in JSON format for transfer, changing your consent to news and your communication language, and an erasure request. - By email to [privacy email]: for anything else, e.g. correcting your details. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We reply within one month at the latest.

What happens with an erasure request: - For warranties that have expired, been locked or been voided, the personal details are anonymised, as described in section 6. - For a warranty that is still valid, the details are needed to honour it, so we keep them until it expires (Art. 17(3) GDPR). Your consent to news and offers is withdrawn immediately. If you wish to give up the warranty so that your details are erased earlier, please contact us.

Complaints: If you believe the processing breaches the law, you can lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (Kifisias 1–3, 115 23 Athens, Greece, www.dpa.gr) or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence. We would appreciate it if you contacted us first so that we can resolve it.

10. Automated decisions

We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing and we do not carry out profiling. Some steps happen automatically, according to the warranty terms you accepted: - calculating the Service and expiry dates, - the reminders, - locking the warranty when a Service is not registered within the deadline.

You can always contact us so that your case is reviewed by a member of our team. Service registrations and repair or replacement requests are always reviewed by our team.
